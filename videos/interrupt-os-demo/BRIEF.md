## Intent

Create an energetic technical product demonstration that defines Interrupt OS in
the opening seconds, follows one continuous real Android journey, and ends with a
complete architecture walkthrough and exact product restatement. The phone remains
anchored on the left throughout so the film reads as one live system rather than a
sequence of presentation slides.

## Assets

- Real Android emulator recordings — the primary proof of product behavior.
- Current SerpApi result data — the source of all price, fare, and evidence visuals.
- Interrupt OS application UI — home, activity, evidence, and feedback surfaces.
- ElevenLabs narration — Ruhaan Social Media Explainer Style using Eleven v4.
- ElevenLabs instrumental score — light, forward-moving, and mixed beneath speech.

## Customizations

- Use notification cards as recurring objects that move through evidence and decision states.
- Give the real app footage generous screen time inside a high-quality Android device frame.
- Use one clean notification sound for the earned interruption; keep the failed interruption silent.
- Show the live QA harness and provider trace to prove the decisions were not predetermined.

## Notes

- No talking head, stock footage, fake testimonials, or invented performance metrics.
- Avoid repeated "not this, but that" constructions in narration.
- Avoid brittle rules, thresholds, regex demonstrations, or hardcoded expected decisions.
- SerpApi is invoked at runtime as evidence inside a broader attention-decision system.
- The application should appear production-grade; do not call it a demo, concept, or proof of concept inside the film.
