Interrupt OS

Every notification should earn the right to interrupt.

The film opens with a live travel fare that fails current-market verification, followed
by a genuine exceptional fare that earns an alert. It then shows preference feedback,
critical pass-through, the real Android application, the agentic evidence pipeline,
and the runtime QA harness.

Product language from the application:
- Working quietly
- Checked
- Kept quiet
- Action needed
- Should alert
- Keep quiet next time
- Current evidence
- Recent decisions

Decision actions:
- preserve
- hold
- replace silently
- replace with an alert

Evidence providers:
- SerpApi Google Shopping
- SerpApi Google Product
- SerpApi Google Flights
- SerpApi Google Flights Deals
